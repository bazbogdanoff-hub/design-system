# ChartLegendGroup

A chart's `ChartLegend` on its own small `Tile` (owner, 2026-09-29):
- corners `md` (8);
- padding 8 top and bottom, 12 at the sides;
- full width, and as tall as the legend.

Use it for a legend under the plot, when the chart card is too narrow for
the legend in its header row. The first use is the Tasks page's Due runway.
Props are `ChartLegend`'s (`items`).
